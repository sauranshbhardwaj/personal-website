# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary visitors are founders, investors, recruiters, and other builders who want a quick read on Sauransh Bhardwaj's current direction, proof of execution, and ways to contact him.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio should serve as a concise personal homepage. It should make Sauransh feel like a high-agency builder with real product mileage across AI, education, enterprise systems, and public-good projects, while staying simple enough to read in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Plainspoken, ambitious, and useful. The tone should feel direct and calm, with evidence doing the work instead of decorative self-promotion.

## Anti-references

Avoid resume pages, startup-pitch clutter, animated personal-brand templates, oversized hero sections, generic gradients, card grids, and visual effects that distract from the writing.

## Design Principles

- Put proof inside the prose.
- Keep the page quieter than the achievements.
- Favor credibility over polish-for-polish's-sake.
- Make links obvious without turning the page into a directory.
- Preserve a strong first impression on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, strong contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, generous line height, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. The site should be readable without JavaScript.
